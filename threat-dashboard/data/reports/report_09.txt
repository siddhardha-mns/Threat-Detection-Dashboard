Victims were enticed by an urgent delivery notification to enable VBA macros in a downloaded Microsoft Word document titled shipping_manifest.doc. Upon execution, the embedded macro bypassed AMSI scanning and invoked a PowerShell cradle retrieving second-stage payloads from 198.51.100.12. The malicious document matched MD5 hash 7d793037a0760186574b0282f2f435e7 and established autostart scheduled tasks.
