Attackers gained entry through compromised VPN credentials and quickly deployed Rust-based BlackCat ransomware across internal hypervisors. Before encrypting volumes with AES-256 and dropping ransom demands, the actors exfiltrated 45GB of corporate data to remote drop server 203.0.113.205 and cloud portal sync-telemetry-backup.org.
