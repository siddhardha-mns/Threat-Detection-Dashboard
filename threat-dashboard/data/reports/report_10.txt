Threat actors with low-privileged container access leveraged CVE-2022-0847, known as Dirty Pipe, to overwrite arbitrary read-only host files including /etc/passwd. This immediately granted root shell privileges without requiring authentication or administrator approval. Exploitation artifacts observed on the victim filesystem carried SHA256 01ba4719c80b6fe911b091a7c05124b64eeece964e09c058ef8f9805daca546b and connected out to 203.0.113.67.
